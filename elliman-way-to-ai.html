What the Book Will Cover

  1. What changed in AI this year, in plain language, and what it means for a listing, a buyer and a closing.
  2. AI skills and commands: reusable instructions that make any AI work the same way every time.
  3. A prompt library for an agent's daily work: CMAs, listing presentations, follow-up, market updates, client email and contract review.
  4. The Elliman standard: brand voice, Fair Housing guardrails, and what client data never goes into a public AI tool.
  5. The bridge to Elli and Elius: when to move a task from a general AI into Elliman's platform, step by step.

Every prompt will be written by an Elliman agent and tested on real listings and real clients before it goes in the book. A draft sample page follows.

Draft sample · ListingsThe Elliman Way to AI
The Listing Appointment Brief
Walk into every listing appointment prepared in ten minutes, not two hours.
Copy this into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok
You are my listing presentation strategist. I am a Douglas Elliman agent meeting a seller at [ADDRESS] in [NEIGHBORHOOD] on [DATE]. The home has [BEDS / BATHS / SQUARE FEET / KEY FEATURES]. Here are recent comparable sales: [PASTE 3–5 COMPS]. Give me:
  1. Three reasons this home will sell, based on its features and the comps.
  2. The two objections this seller is most likely to raise, and how I answer each.
  3. A plain-language pricing conversation I can have at the kitchen table.
  4. A 90-second opening I can say out loud.
Keep the tone confident, warm and professional. Describe the neighborhood by its homes, amenities and location only.
The Elliman standard
Never paste a seller's financial details, ID numbers or contract into a public AI. Describe places, never the people who live there.
Take it into Elli
Ask Elli for a branded market report and pricing strategy for the address. Bring both to the appointment.
Try it now
Run it before your next appointment. Aim to finish in under ten minutes.
