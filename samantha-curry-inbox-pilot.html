Curry Inbox Engine

30-Day Paid Pilot — AI reply drafts, written into your Outlook, reviewed and sent only by you
Prepared for Samantha Curry  •  The Bristol, Palm Beach

Samantha — you and Heather are two people carrying a four-person workload, and most of it lives in Outlook. Hiring takes months you don't have. This pilot puts a system into your existing inbox workflow that drafts replies to your most repetitive email in your voice — so the two of you review and send instead of composing from scratch.

Built-In Safeguards

  • It cannot send email. The system is never granted send permission — technically incapable, not just policy. Every email is sent by a human: you or Heather.
  • Fair Housing screen on 100% of drafts. Every draft passes an independent Fair Housing review before it's written. If it fails, the draft is never created.
  • Your email is not stored. Message content is processed in memory and never retained — no bodies, no attachments, ever.
  • One-email kill switch. Send an email with PAUSE in the subject and all drafting stops within one 5-minute cycle, confirmed within one business hour.
  • You own the mailbox. Track 2 runs on a Microsoft 365 account on your own domain, owned by you — not by Bogen.ai, not by anyone else.

How We Measure Success

No vague "do you like it" metrics. The pilot is scored on five numbers, tracked weekly:

  • Drafts created per week
  • Percentage of drafts you actually send — the number that matters most
  • Hours per week returned to you and Heather
  • Bad-draft incidents (target: zero)
  • Fair Housing blocks (each one reviewed — that's the screen doing its job)

The Fine Print, In Plain English

2. Fair Housing and your license

Every draft passes an automated Fair Housing screen before it is written, and drafts the system is not confident about are simply not created. The screen is a risk-reduction tool — not legal advice and not a compliance guarantee. As the licensed real estate professional, you retain full responsibility for Fair Housing, Florida license-law, and advertising compliance, including your signature and disclosures when sending from your own domain.

3. Your brokerage's policies are yours to confirm

You are responsible for confirming that Douglas Elliman's policies permit redirecting or forwarding mail from your corporate mailbox and replying from your own domain.
