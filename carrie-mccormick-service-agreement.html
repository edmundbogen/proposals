Between Dave T Productions, LLC d/b/a Bogen.ai and Carrie McCormick · Draft v3 · July 24, 2026

This ListingStory Service Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the date of last signature below (the "Effective Date") by and between Dave T Productions, LLC, a Florida limited liability company doing business as Bogen.ai, with its principal place of business in Boca Raton, Florida ("Provider"), and Carrie McCormick, an individual real estate licensee affiliated with @properties Christie's International Real Estate ("Client"). Provider and Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

1.Definitions

  • "Platform" means Provider's ListingStory software system, including all code, templates, page layouts, designs, prompts, workflows, integrations, documentation, and improvements, whether existing before or developed during this Agreement.
  • "Client Instance" means the Client-branded deployment of the Platform operated by Provider for Client.
  • "Client Data" means information submitted to the Client Instance by Client, Client's team, or property sellers, including seller contact information and property information.
  • "Client Brand" means Client's name, likeness, logos, trademarks, brand assets, and biographical content.
  • "Generated Content" means text, documents, pages, and reports produced by the Client Instance for Client (e.g., Property Stories, feature sheets, listing-page content, analytics reports), excluding the Platform's templates, layouts, designs, and code.
  • "AI Services" means artificial-intelligence models and services selected by Provider, which may include services from Anthropic, OpenAI, or other vendors. Provider may select, combine, and substitute AI Services and other subprocessors as set out in Section 8.2, provided the quality of the Services is not materially degraded.
  • "Security Incident" means actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, modification, disclosure, destruction, or loss of availability of, Client Data in Provider's possession or control (including encrypted data where the means of decryption is compromised).
  • "Client Dependencies" means the items and cooperation Client must provide under Section 6.4.
  • "Third-Party Services" means the third-party services described in Section 9.
  • "Order Form" means a scope-and-fees exhibit executed by both Parties under this Agreement. Exhibit A (Module 1) is the initial Order Form. Additional modules require a new Order Form; informal communications (including email or text) do not modify scope.

2.Services

2.1 Provider will build, host, operate, and support the Client Instance and deliver the services described in each executed Order Form (the "Services").

2.2 Change orders. Scope changes, additional features, and new modules are added only by a written Order Form or amendment signed by both Parties.

2.3 Non-exclusivity. The Services are provided on a non-exclusive basis. Provider may develop, market, and provide the same or similar services, systems, and content frameworks to other clients, provided Provider will never use Client Brand or Client Data for any other client. Any territorial exclusivity, if agreed, will be set out in a separately priced addendum.

2.4 Service levels. Provider will perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner and will use commercially reasonable efforts to meet the target turnaround times described in an Order Form. Target times are goals, not guarantees, and are conditioned on Client Dependencies (Section 6) and Third-Party Services (Section 9). No service credits apply. Client's exclusive remedy for breach of the performance warranty in this Section is re-performance of the affected Service following written notice given within thirty (30) days of the affected Service.

2.5 Right to refuse. Provider may decline to generate, and may suspend or remove from the Client Instance, content it reasonably believes to be unlawful, discriminatory, infringing, or deceptive, without breach of this Agreement, and will promptly notify Client of any such action.

3.Fees & Payment

3.1 Fees for each module are stated in its Order Form. For Module 1: a one-time build fee of $3,500 (50% deposit on signature of this Agreement; 50% on Acceptance as defined in Exhibit A) and a monthly service fee of $395 beginning on the Acceptance date, billed monthly in advance.

3.2 Method. Provider invoices via Stripe; payment by ACH or card. Client authorizes automatic monthly charge of recurring fees to a payment method on file.

3.3 Late payment. Amounts unpaid ten (10) days past due accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum lawful rate, whichever is less. Provider may suspend the Services (including hosting, sync, and generation) on five (5) days' written notice until cured. Suspension is not termination and does not relieve accrued fees.

3.4 Fee changes. Recurring fees are fixed for the Initial Term of the applicable Order Form and may be adjusted thereafter on thirty (30) days' written notice. Recurring fees do not vary with Client's listing volume within the fair-use assumption stated in the applicable Order Form.

3.5 Termination before Acceptance. Client may terminate an Order Form for convenience before Acceptance on written notice. The deposit is applied to reservation of capacity, discovery, configuration, and initial development and is earned as those activities occur; Provider will refund any portion of the deposit materially exceeding the value of work completed and third-party costs committed as of the termination date. Deliverables completed as of termination are provided to Client under Section 5.

3.6 Taxes. Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes, which are Client's responsibility (excluding taxes on Provider's income).

4.Term & Termination

4.1 Term. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues while any Order Form is active (the "Term"). Each Order Form runs for an initial term of twelve (12) months from its Acceptance date (the "Initial Term"), then renews month-to-month.

4.2 Termination for convenience. After the Initial Term, either Party may terminate an Order Form or this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice.

4.3 Termination for cause. Either Party may terminate if the other materially breaches and fails to cure within fifteen (15) days of written notice. Provider may terminate immediately for non-payment continuing ten (10) days after a suspension under Section 3.3.

4.4 Effect of termination. On termination: (a) the Client Instance and listing pages are taken offline following a transition period of up to fifteen (15) days if requested by Client in writing within five (5) days of the termination notice (recurring fees continue pro rata during transition, prepaid; Provider may decline transition where termination results from Client's unlawful content or security misuse); (b) Provider exports Client Data and Generated Content per Section 8.5; (c) each Party ceases public use of the other's marks except as separately approved in writing under Section 13; (d) accrued payment obligations survive. Sections 5, 7, 8, 10–12, and 14–16 survive termination.

6.4 Dependencies. Client will provide in a timely manner: Monday.com account access, brand assets and bio/team copy, property photographs, seller cooperation, draft approvals, and scheduled calls. Provider's target timelines are extended day-for-day by delays in Client Dependencies.

6.5 Seller-facing terms. Client will direct sellers to complete the intake truthfully and will not remove or bypass the intake's privacy notice, consent, and accuracy-attestation steps. 8.1 Use. Provider uses Client Data solely to provide the Services under Client's instructions as documented in this Agreement and the Order Forms. Provider will not sell Client Data, will not use seller personal information for Provider's own marketing, and will not use identifiable Client Data to train AI models or improve unrelated products.

8.2 Subprocessors. Client authorizes the subprocessors listed in Exhibit B. Provider will contractually require each recipient of Illinois residents' personal information to implement and maintain reasonable security measures protecting the information from unauthorized access, acquisition, destruction, use, modification, or disclosure, as required by 815 ILCS 530/45(b), and to notify Provider promptly of any Security Incident. Provider will give Client at least fifteen (15) days' notice of a material subprocessor change (except emergency replacements, with prompt notice after); if the change materially increases privacy or security risk, Client may object in writing and, failing resolution, terminate the affected Order Form on notice.

8.3 Security. Provider will implement and maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data, consistent with the Illinois Personal Information Protection Act (815 ILCS 530), including access controls and authentication on administrative dashboards, encryption in transit, least-privilege access, and logging of administrative access.

8.4 Prohibited data. The intake is designed so that property access details (e.g., codes) are not collected through the form; sellers are directed to convey any such information to Client directly, and Client will not instruct sellers otherwise. Client will not submit, and will direct sellers not to submit, Social Security or government-ID numbers, financial account credentials, medical or biometric information, children's data, alarm codes, or immigration information through the Services.

8.5 Export and deletion. On termination or written request, Provider will export Client Data and Generated Content in commercially standard formats (CSV/JSON for data; PDF/HTML/text for documents and pages) within fifteen (15) days, and will delete Client Data from active systems within sixty (60) days of a written deletion request, and from routine backups within ninety (90) days on cycle, except as required by law. Deletion of data required to operate the Services may require termination of the affected Order Form, and Provider will so advise before acting on the request.

8.6 Incident response. Provider will investigate promptly and notify Client within forty-eight (48) hours of discovering an actual or reasonably suspected Security Incident, provide rolling factual updates and a root-cause summary, preserve relevant evidence, and reasonably cooperate on legally required notifications. This contractual reporting obligation is independent of, and does not enlarge or reduce, any statutory consumer-notification threshold or deadline, including under 815 ILCS 530/10. Neither Party will notify affected individuals on the other's behalf without coordination unless legally required. Costs of responding to a Security Incident are borne by the Party whose breach of this Agreement or negligence caused it; otherwise each Party bears its own costs.

8.7 Confidentiality. Each Party will protect the other's non-public business, technical, security, pricing, and seller information with reasonable care, use it only to perform this Agreement, and disclose it only to personnel and advisors who need it and are bound to confidentiality. Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes publicly available without breach, was rightfully known before disclosure, is independently developed without use of the other Party's information, or is rightfully received from a third party without duty of confidence — except that seller personal information remains protected regardless. Compelled disclosures are permitted with prompt notice where lawful. Breach of this Section may cause irreparable harm, and the injured Party may seek injunctive relief without posting bond. This Section survives termination for three (3) years (indefinitely for seller personal information).

Exhibit A — Order Form No. 1: Module 1, "The Reveal"

Scope: (1) branded seller intake form with privacy notice, consent, and seller accuracy attestation; (2) Monday.com synchronization of intake records (field mapping agreed at setup); (3) AI drafting engine producing draft listing remarks and social caption per intake, with automated fair-housing screening aid and a Client approval workflow that records the approver, final text, and timestamp — nothing auto-publishes; (4) branded feature-sheet PDF per intake; (5) branded listing page per property launched through the system (Client bio/team content, property information, photographs via upload link), hosted by Provider; (6) basic listing-page traffic metrics and a shareable activity report.

Onboarding support: white-glove support for Client's first three (3) listings, up to two (2) working sessions each.

Target timeline: production hardening and field mapping in week 1; photo pipeline, analytics, end-to-end sample test, and go-live in week 2 — subject to Client Dependencies (Section 6.4).

Acceptance: the earlier of (a) Client's written approval of a successful end-to-end test with one sample property (intake → Property Story → feature sheet → listing page → Monday.com sync) in a current version of Chrome or Safari; (b) five (5) business days after Provider notifies Client the system is live and available for testing, unless Client identifies a Material Defect in writing; or (c) Client's first production use with a real seller. A Material Defect is a reproducible failure that substantially prevents one of the core workflow steps listed in (a); Provider has five (5) business days to cure and re-tender, and the Acceptance clock restarts on re-tender. Minor cosmetic items do not delay Acceptance and are handled in ordinary support. Provider may invoice on Acceptance notwithstanding open non-material items. Long-stop: if Client has not completed testing or provided outstanding Client Dependencies within thirty (30) days of Provider's go-live notice, Acceptance is deemed to occur, or Provider may terminate this Order Form under Section 3.5 economics. The long-stop period is tolled while a timely reported Material Defect remains uncured.

Fees: Build fee $3,500 — $1,750 on signature of this Agreement; $1,750 on Acceptance, net 7 days. Monthly service fee $395, commencing on the Acceptance date, billed monthly in advance.

Fair-use assumption: the monthly fee includes up to fifteen (15) new listings launched through the system per calendar month; sustained volume above this level is addressed by good-faith Order Form amendment, and no additional fee applies unless agreed in writing. Provider may defer launches exceeding fifteen (15) in a calendar month until an amendment is agreed.

Initial Term: twelve (12) months from Acceptance, then month-to-month.
