AI Advisory Retainer

Samantha — the Sprint proved what this looks like when it works: you call, we build, you use it the same day. This agreement makes that the standing arrangement. Edmund and Eytan become your AI team on retainer — the people you call for a valuation, a pricing question, a tool that isn't behaving, the next system you need built, or an hour of one-on-one training for you or Heather.

The Engagement

What You Get

Included every 30 days

  • Valuation and pricing support. CMAs, comp analysis, and pricing scenarios for your listings and buyers, using the Valuation Cockpit and our tools — turned around on your timeline, including same-day when a deal calls for it.
  • Your tech go-between. One call or email to us instead of five to vendors. We set up, troubleshoot, and connect your AI tools (Claude, Wispr Flow, MLS connections, and whatever comes next) and deal with the technical side on your behalf.
  • Training and one-on-one work. Hands-on sessions for you and Heather — screen-share or in person — on the tools you actually use, at the pace you need.
  • Ongoing AI advisory. A standing sounding board for where AI fits in your business, what to adopt, what to ignore, and what to build next.
  • Integration and ongoing builds. We keep integrating what's already underway — including your automated voicemail system — and continue building additional valuation systems and tools as your business calls for them, under this same approach. Everything we've built — the Valuation Cockpit, the voicemail system, and every future build — stays hosted, updated, and working.
