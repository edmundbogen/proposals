The Decision

Jay, Elliman is investing in AI with Elli and Elius. The harder part is getting agents to use AI well, and consistently. I'm proposing a small Florida pilot to test a practical way to do that before any larger commitment.

Edmund Bogen

The questionIn Microsoft and LinkedIn's 2024 survey of 31,000 knowledge workers, 78% of AI users brought their own AI tools to work, and only 39% had received AI training from their company. That survey did not measure Elliman agents. It points to a question worth testing in Florida: how our agents use AI today, and how consistently that use reflects Elliman's standards for brand, client information and Fair Housing.
The pilotA hands-on program for agents in selected Florida offices and on Zoom, with a preview of the book's core material. Agents learn in the AI they already use, apply Elliman's standards, and see when Elli is the right tool.
What we learnWhether agents attend, finish the work, keep using it, and move tasks into Elli, measured against a baseline we agree on before the first session.
After the pilotA short written report and a decision: expand, adjust or stop. Expansion could include the book, The Elliman Way to AI, and a wider Florida rollout.

The Listing Appointment Brief
Prepare for a listing appointment in less time, with every number checked.
Works in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok

Act as my listing presentation coach. I'm preparing for a listing appointment. The property is a four-bedroom, three-bath single-family pool home of about 2,800 square feet, built in 1998, in a gated community in Boca Raton, with an updated kitchen and a lake view. Below are recent comparable sales from the MLS, with addresses removed: [PASTE: beds, baths, square feet, sale price, sale date, key features].

Give me:
  1. Three strengths to emphasize, based only on the home's features and these comps.
  2. Two pricing questions the seller is likely to ask, and a plain-language way to walk through the comps.
  3. A 60-second opening for the appointment.

Describe the property and its features only, never the people who live nearby or who should buy it. If the comps don't support a point, say so.

The Elliman standard
Leave out names, contact details, finances and anything from a signed agreement. Share only MLS data you are permitted to use, with addresses removed.
Verify before you use
AI can be wrong. Check every comp, number and claim against the MLS and public records. The pricing opinion is yours, not the AI's.
Fair Housing
Describe the home, not people. Avoid phrases such as "perfect for families," "safe neighborhood" or "ideal for young professionals."
Where Elli fits
Where Elli is available to you, use it for the Elliman-branded report you share with the seller.

If the Pilot Works

The pilot ends with a short written report and a decision meeting: expand, adjust or stop. If Elliman chooses to expand, Phase 2 could include:

  • The book, The Elliman Way to AI, as the course material for every participating agent.
  • A wider Florida rollout of the program to more offices.
  • An annual option to add copies, offices or regions beyond Florida, at Elliman's decision.
